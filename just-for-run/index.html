Private by design

No account. No ads. No tracking.

Your activities belong to you. Here's exactly where they go.

Location, only while recording

GPS is used during an activity and never otherwise. “Always” access is optional and only keeps a recording going with the phone locked.

On your iPhone, in your iCloud

Activities live on your device and sync only through your own private iCloud. The developer can't see them.

Links carry the run

A shared run is packed into the link after “#”, which browsers never send to the server.

Profile is opt-in

Off by default. Only what you choose is published, and turning it off deletes it.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. No subscription, no ads, no in-app purchases.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. GPS doesn't need a data connection, and every point is saved on your phone as it arrives. The map tiles may not load while you're offline, but your route is recorded and drawn as soon as you're back online. Sync catches up on its own.

Will it drain my battery?

Location is only used while an activity is recording. When you stop, GPS stops.

Can I bring my activities from the old version?

Yes. Updating from 1.0 moves every activity, route, tag and setting across the first time you open the new version. JSON backup and restore works with every version.

Who can see a shared link?

Anyone you send it to. The run is inside the link itself, so there's nothing stored online to delete; if you want an address that can be taken down, use the public profile, which you can switch off at any time.
