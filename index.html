FAQ

Questions

Is Perch really free?

Yes. No trial, no subscription, no account.

My Mac doesn't have a notch. Does it work?

Yes. On screens without a notch, Perch sits as a small pill at the top centre of the screen. You can also open it with ⌥N on any Mac.

Do I need an iPhone?

No. Everything works on your Mac alone. If you have one, Perch for iPhone (coming soon to the App Store) keeps your plan in sync through your own iCloud.

How do updates work?

Perch checks for a new version once a day and asks before installing. Every update is signed, and you can turn checks off in Settings.

macOS warns me when I open it.

Perch is notarized by Apple, so you should only see the usual “downloaded from the internet” question. Make sure it came from this page, drag it to Applications and open it from there.

How do I uninstall it?

Quit Perch from its menu, then drag it from Applications to the Bin.
