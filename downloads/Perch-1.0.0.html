Perch for Mac — a planner that lives in your notch (or a pill at the top of the screen on Macs without one).
  • Today and Upcoming: quick add that understands "call mom tomorrow 5pm !high #family every week ~15m", Top 3, priorities, tags, repeating tasks, subtasks, links, snooze, drag between days, overdue "spread over the week".
  • Notes: Markdown notes with live styling, slash commands, a daily note, and checklists that are real tasks both ways. Optional two-way sync with a "Perch" folder in Apple Notes.
  • Focus: ring timer, custom lengths, focus/break cycles, chime, session strip.
  • Learn: learning plans with pace, spaced-repetition recall cards.
  • Goals: monthly and weekly goals that count tasks, focus hours or lessons by themselves, or are logged by hand, with an even-pace marker; weekly review.
  • ⌘K palette (⌥Space anywhere), Services capture from any app, optional Clips clipboard history (⌥⇧V), standup and end-of-day review, meeting notes from calendar events.
  • Calendar and Reminders (optional): today's events, two-way Reminders sync with priority and repeats.
  • AI agents: 27 MCP tools for Claude Code, Claude Desktop and Cursor.
  • iCloud sync (optional) with the Perch iPhone app.
  • Pastel and Classic themes.
    • Perch for iPhone — Today, Upcoming, Notes and Goals, synced with your Mac through your private iCloud. Everything works on the Mac alone; iPhone, iCloud, Reminders and Apple Notes are optional.
