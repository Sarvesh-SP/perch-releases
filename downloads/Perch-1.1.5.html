Perch for Mac
  • Money › Overview: what's left to spend today, a chart of the month's spending against an even pace to your plan, a ring for how much of the plan is used, quick stats (average a day, no-spend days, vs last month, busiest day), where your money went and the latest transactions.
  • Money › Budget: each category as a tile with its colour, spent of plan, what's left or over, and a marker for where you should be today. Click a tile to change its plan.
  • Runs: this week's distance with the change on last week, your streak and the week's bars; your month, year, longest and best pace; and activity cards with a route drawing and the key numbers, like "Morning Run · 6.26 km · 5:25 /km".
