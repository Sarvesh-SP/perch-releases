Perch for Mac
  • Money tab (optional): your Karo Budget data on the Mac. Safe to spend today, budget against actual with plans you can edit, and add, edit or delete transactions. Works on the Mac alone; with iCloud on it stays in step with Karo Budget on iPhone.
  • Runs tab (optional): what you record with just for run. Weekly chart, totals and streaks, filtered by activity (Running, Cycling…), and a big map window for any run with the route coloured by pace, splits, elevation and a share link.
  • Beside the closed notch (optional): money left today and your run streak.
  • ⌘K palette: "spent 250 coffee #food" logs an expense.
  • Claude: new tools to log expenses, read spending and budgets, and summarise runs by activity.
