Perch for Mac
  • The notch no longer gets in the way of browser tabs. By default Perch now opens when you rest the pointer on the notch for a moment. Passing over it, sliding along a full-screen app's tabs, or clicking something under it no longer opens it.
  • Choose how Perch opens in Settings › General: pause on the notch, touch the notch, click the notch, or keyboard only (⌥N).
  • Money › Transactions fits the panel again: filters on one row, totals and categories above the list. The statement review's lines fit as well.
