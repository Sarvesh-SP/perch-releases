Questions

Is it free?

Yes. No subscription, no ads, no in-app purchases.

Does it work outside India?

It's built around rupees and Indian number formatting (₹1,20,000), but the budgeting works anywhere.

I used the old version. Is my data safe?

Yes. Transactions, monthly plans, groups, categories, splits and settled pairs all move across automatically the first time you open the new version, and older CSV backups still restore.

Does it read my bank SMS or connect to my bank?

No. You add transactions yourself, or import a statement through an AI chat of your choice. Karo Budget has no access to your bank.

What does “left for today” mean?

Your income for the month, minus what you've planned and what you've already spent, divided across the days remaining. It updates as you go.
