Privacy, plainly.

Applies to Karo Budget for iPhone. Last updated 9 November 2026.

Karo Budget doesn't collect your data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no connection to your bank. The developer can't see your transactions, plans or anything else you put in the app.

Where your budget lives

  • On your iPhone. Transactions, monthly plans, groups, categories, splits, savings settings and your salary (if you enter it) are stored on your device.
  • In your private iCloud, if iCloud is on for the app, so your devices stay in step. This is your own iCloud database, run by Apple; only your Apple ID can read it. Perch for Mac's optional Money tab reads the same private database.
  • In backups you make. CSV files you export go wherever you save them.

Importing a statement

Statement import works through an AI chat of your choice. Karo Budget gives you a prompt containing your category names and today's date; you copy it into the chat yourself, along with your statement, and paste the answer back. Karo Budget doesn't send your statement or the prompt anywhere, and isn't connected to any AI service. What you share in that chat is covered by that service's own privacy policy.

Network

Apart from Apple's iCloud sync, Karo Budget makes no network connections of its own.

Crash reports

Crash and hang reports come from iOS only if you've chosen to share them with developers in Settings › Privacy.

Deleting your data

Settings in the app can reset a month or a year. Deleting the app removes its data from the device; iCloud data can be removed in Settings › your name › iCloud › Manage Storage.

Children

Karo Budget doesn't collect personal information from anyone, of any age.

Questions

Open an issue at github.com/Sarvesh-SP/perch-releases. If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too.
